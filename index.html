Slat Team Util

Slat Team Util is a private Discord bot used internally by Slat's video editing team. It tracks projects, editor assignments, deliveries, posting schedules, and weekly payment records inside the team's Discord server.

It is not a public product and does not offer sign-ups. Optionally, the team's administrators connect Google accounts with read-only access so the bot can:

  • read the names and creation dates of project folders in the team's Google Drive, to register new projects;
  • read the upload list of the team's YouTube channel, to fill in posting schedules.

The bot never uploads, edits, moves, shares, or deletes anything in Google Drive or YouTube.
